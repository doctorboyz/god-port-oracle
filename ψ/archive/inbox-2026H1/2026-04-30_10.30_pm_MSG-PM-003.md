# Task: Monte Carlo Simulation for Strategy Robustness

## Context

G4 forward test shows WR=43.3% vs backtest WR=53% — a 10pp gap that suggests possible overfitting or regime dependency. Before adjusting the strategy or proceeding to G5 (demo trading), we need to understand how robust the current config actually is.

## Objective

Run Monte Carlo simulation on the Phase 1.5 best config to measure:

1. **Equity curve robustness** — 1000+ randomized trade-order simulations
2. **WR distribution** — what's the 5th percentile WR? (if 5th% > 45%, strategy is statistically sound)
3. **MaxDD distribution** — what's the worst-case MaxDD at 95th percentile?
4. **PF distribution** — what's the 5th percentile PF?
5. **Ruin probability** — % of simulations where equity drops below 50% of starting capital
